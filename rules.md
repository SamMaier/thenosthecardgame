"4 Player game. This game takes place over 6 ""days"" (rounds). To start, everyone is dealt 7 cards. Then, each day has two phases - the drawing phase, and the playing phase. At the start of the day, players get 7 energy cubes, and the top Daily Condition card is revealed.

Trunk: the main deck, to be shuffled before the start of the game. If it runs out during the game, shuffle the discard pile and use it as your Trunk.
Suitcase: a set of 4 face up cards players choose from. Whenever a card is taken from the Suitcase, its spot is immediately refilled from the Trunk, so any player picking more than one card will see the replacement before picking again. The Suitcase stays visible during the whole game and is only refreshed when a card is taken or when a player ""Unpacks"", where the whole Suitcase is discarded and refilled from the Trunk. 

Drawing phase: One card at a time, we go around the circle, and each player takes one card from the Suitcase. A player may Unpack any time before they take their card at the cost of -1 Fun. Once each player has had 3 turns to pick, we go the playing phase.

Playing phase: players go around, taking turns playing one card from their hand, paying the amount of Energy the cards cost. Cards are all played left to right, and anything related to Energy happens immediately. Once a player no longer wishes to play a card, they ""go to bed"" and can no longer play cards. The first to go to bed becomes the starting player the next day. Once all players have gone to bed, it is ""end of day"", and Fun is scored.

Fun is scored for each card individually, from left to right, and any bonuses or effects from other cards that affect this card should happen left to right. Any cards not played from your hand are kept for the next day. All cards played today are discarded, and any cards marked for ""Tomorrow"" get moved above your playing area, to indicate that they are now on the ""Tomorrow"" action. Active ""Tomorrow"" cards played yesterday do not count as cards played ""previously"", ""today"", or ""before"", and are not available as targets of any card played today. Active ""Tomorrow"" cards are scored before today's cards and are discarded at the end of the day.

All effects trigger exclusively with cards visible. If the card is not visible when resolving Energy cost or scoring Fun, it does not apply. So, ""next"", ""after"" no longer applies after the card is discarded, and ""previous"" does not apply if the previous card was discarded.

The player with the most fun at the end of the 6 days wins.

Quick Clarifications:
Energy for a card may never go negative.
There is no hand size limit.
If a card has a restriction such that it cannot be played, but another card plays it for you (whether from your hand, the Suitcase, or the Trunk), the card is returned to your hand at no cost.
Copying another card works as if you had printed an exact copy of the copied card and played it yourself, except the Energy cost is possibly different. All your cards trigger on it as normal. Note that you may only copy a card that you can legally play in the slot you are playing the copier.
When resolving other cards effects on the current card (whether determining the Energy to pay, or the Fun scored), card effects happen left to right. For example, say you are playing a 4 Energy card, but your leftmost card will make it cost double Energy, and your next card says it'll cost -1 Energy, it costs (4x2)-1=7. Then, at end of day, say your relevant cards that affect the scoring of this card are (in order) +3 Fun , Double Fun, (the card, worth 1 Fun), +4 Fun, this card would score ((1+3)x2)+4=12 Fun.
Some cards mention using an Energy cube to indicate on another card. This is just to remember which card you picked, it does not cost an extra Energy, just use an extra cube on it."
